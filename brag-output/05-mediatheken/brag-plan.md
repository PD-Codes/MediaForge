# Brag Plan: MediaForge — Folge 5/5: Alle Mediatheken

> Final copy: English (changed mid-run at the user's request). The rendered video and composition-brief.md hold the English text; German lines below are the original draft.

## What is this app?
MediaForge gives every media type its own library ("Mediathek"): Filme & Serien, eBooks and Comics today, with Manga and Musik marked "bald" — each with a built-in player or reader, plus Jellyfin/Plex integration.

## The angle
"Eine App. Jede Mediathek." A cinematic tour from the library hub into three worlds: the web player, the eBook reader, the comic reader.

## Hook (0–2.6s)
Five library cards fan out from the centre — Filme & Serien, eBooks, Comics, Manga (bald), Musik (bald) — line: "Eine App. Jede Mediathek."

## Key moments
- Filme & Serien: web player frame with "Intro überspringen" button, source picker listing hosters with language/resolution/response time, 10 s jump.
- eBooks: one book card with format badges EPUB · MOBI · AZW3 · PDF ("ein Buch, ein Eintrag"), then the reader in Sepia palette, "Weiterlesen, wo du warst — pro Buch".
- Comics: series → issues; double-page spread opens; "ComicInfo.xml" tag; "Original bleibt unverändert".
- Jellyfin / Plex pill: everything shows up there too (.nfo metadata).

## Outro
"Alles an einem Ort." → logo + "Kostenlos. Self-hosted. Open Source."

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: premium trailer — big type, wide moves, deliberate reveals
- Interpretation: "object becomes the world" transitions: a library card grows into its library.
