# Brag Plan: MediaForge — Folge 4/5: Kalender

> Final copy: English (changed mid-run at the user's request). The rendered video and composition-brief.md hold the English text; German lines below are the original draft.

## What is this app?
MediaForge's Calendar shows upcoming episodes and releases (from TMDB/CineInfo, your AutoSync jobs, Crunchyroll simulcasts) in Monat, Woche and Agenda views with search and media-type filters, a detail modal on every entry, and an iCal subscription feed for Google/Apple Calendar, Thunderbird or DAVx5.

## The angle
"Nie wieder verpassen, wann's weitergeht." The calendar fills itself; you just subscribe on your phone.

## Hook (0–2.4s)
A month grid where release chips drop onto days rapidly; line: "Wann kommt die nächste Folge?" → answered by "Steht schon im Kalender."

## Key moments
- Month view (fictional October grid) with Serien (violet) and Filme (blue) chips, "Heute" marker.
- View switcher Monat → Woche → Agenda: same data re-flows into a week strip then an agenda list.
- Filter: "Filme / Reihen / Alle" pills + "Titel filtern…" search narrows the list.
- Detail modal: click an entry → poster block, "S2 · E08", date, overview line, "Auto-Sync aktiv".
- "Kalender-Abo": subscription URL copied → phone calendar app tiles: Google Calendar, Apple Kalender, Thunderbird, DAVx5.

## Outro
"Dein Release-Plan. Automatisch." → logo.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: smooth, tidy, satisfying "things snap into place" feel
- Interpretation: drops and morphs, grid reflows.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920   ## Duration: 22s

## Visual identity
MediaForge tokens; series chips #7c3aed, movie chips #3b82f6 (info), today ring #22c55e. Inter.
