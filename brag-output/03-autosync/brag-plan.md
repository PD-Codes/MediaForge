# Brag Plan: MediaForge — Folge 3/5: Auto-Sync

> Final copy: English (changed mid-run at the user's request). The rendered video and composition-brief.md hold the English text; German lines below are the original draft.

## What is this app?
MediaForge's AutoSync keeps series up to date automatically — on an interval or a weekly schedule, with per-job season/episode filters, a dry run, and "Alle synchronisieren" — so new episodes download themselves.

## The angle
"Neue Folge? Liegt schon da." You go to sleep; the job wakes up on its schedule, finds the new episode and downloads it. Told as a night → morning clock.

## Hook (0–2.5s)
A clock face/time "03:00" ticks; "Du schläfst." then "MediaForge nicht." lands.

## Key moments
- Auto-Sync page: job cards (fictional series) with status pills Läuft / In der Warteschlange / fällig, "Nächste Prüfung in 2 Std", "Zuletzt geprüft".
- Edit sync job: toggle Intervall ↔ Wöchentlich; weekday pills Mo–So, Mi + Sa selected, time 20:00; "Staffeln & Episoden" filter S2 E1–12.
- Dry run: "Probelauf" result panel lists exactly what would be queued — "E07 · E08 würden geladen", nothing queued.
- "Alle synchronisieren" click → cards go Läuft one by one (max two at a time) → notification "2 neue Episoden geladen".
- "Warten (Hold)" card: "Pfad nicht erreichbar" — the sync waits until the path is back and then starts on its own.

## Outro / punchline
"Einmal einrichten. Nie wieder suchen." → logo.

## User flow
Create job + schedule → dry run → automatic download → new episode in queue.

## Tone
- Preset: polished/cinematic hybrid
- Creative direction: night-to-morning product story, calm but driving
- Interpretation: slower moves, longer holds, one big payoff.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920   ## Duration: 22s

## Visual identity
MediaForge tokens (#0d0d14, #16162a, #7c3aed, #2563eb, #f0f0f8, #9191b0, success #22c55e, warning #f59e0b), Inter. Night tint: deeper navy glow at the start, warm violet at the payoff.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with a driving pulse
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1 (energetic, 120 BPM), entered at its strong section: data-media-start 15.02s — only this video uses it
- Cue guidance (track time → video time = track − 15.02): strong cues 16.02→1.00, 17.02→2.00, 20.02→5.00, 23.02→8.00; later beats every 0.5s. Lock "MediaForge nicht." ≈2.0s and "Alle synchronisieren" click ≈ a beat in scene 4.
- Audio-reactive: subtle, the clock/glow brightens with RMS
- SFX: interface/switch for toggles, ui/click for buttons, card-place for status flips, impactBell for the payoff notification. Volume 0.6–0.75.
- Restraint: no ticking-clock gimmick sound loops.

## Storyboard
1. Night hook — 2.6s — "03:00", "Du schläfst." / "MediaForge nicht." → glow flood
2. Jobs grid — 4.2s — 4 job cards with statuses, "Nächste Prüfung" countdown → zoom into one card
3. Schedule — 4.6s — Intervall → Wöchentlich toggle, weekday pills, time, season filter → slide
4. Probelauf — 3.4s — dry run panel lists E07/E08 "würden geladen" with "Nichts wurde eingereiht" → slide
5. Sync all — 4.0s — click "Alle synchronisieren", cards flip to Läuft two at a time, notification "2 neue Episoden" + Hold card → dip
6. Outro — 3.2s — "Einmal einrichten. Nie wieder suchen." + logo

Fictional stand-ins: series titles "Sternenpfad", "Neon Ronin", "Kaltes Echo", "Die letzte Station".
